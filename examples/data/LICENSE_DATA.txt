Pseudobulk responses (mean log1p(CP10k) of treated minus unstimulated CD14 monocytes; 8,513 genes; values rounded
to 8 significant digits) of one donor (donor "0") to IFN-beta, IFN-gamma and TNF-alpha, derived from the public
NCBI GEO series GSE181897 (Hartoularos et al.). NCBI places no restrictions on the use or distribution of GEO data;
submitters may claim rights in their data (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html). Cite GSE181897
when using it. This file is not covered by the MIT licence of the code.
